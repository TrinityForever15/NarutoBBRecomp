# Legal and content policy

This repository is designed to distribute original source code, patches,
non-proprietary numeric metadata, and reproducible procedures only.

## Not included

The repository must not contain game ISOs, XEX files, title updates, DLC, saves,
encryption keys, generated guest C++, extracted shaders, audio, video, fonts,
textures, models, archives, screenshots, GPU traces, or other copyrighted game
content and proprietary-derived artifacts.

The `.gitignore`, contribution policy, and invariant tests provide defense in
depth, but every contributor remains responsible for reviewing their changes.

## User-supplied files

Users must obtain the game legally and prepare the required local data from
their own copy. The project does not provide downloads, keys, or instructions
for defeating copy protection. Local files are expected under
`recomp/fase4/assets` and are never uploaded by supported workflows.
