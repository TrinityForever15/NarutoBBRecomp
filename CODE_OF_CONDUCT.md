# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, technically honest, and welcoming. Discuss evidence and tradeoffs
without attacking people. Assume good intent, credit prior work, and make room
for contributors with different experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
content, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and knowingly submitting copyrighted
or confidential material without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may restrict
participation when behavior threatens the community or the legal safety of the
project. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a
GitHub security advisory or another private contact method published by the
maintainer.

This compact policy is based on the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
